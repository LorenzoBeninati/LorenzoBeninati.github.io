Lorenzo Beninati

PhD student in Mathematics · Université de Toulon

Since 2024, I have been a PhD student in Mathematics at Université de Toulon, within the IMATH laboratory, under the supervision of Yves Aubry and Fabien Herbaut.

My current research interests focus on algebraic geometry over finite fields, in particular on the study of rational points on curves. I am also interested in the study of differential uniformity of polynomials over finite fields.
